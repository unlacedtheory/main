The Genesis: UT Run - 1

Every movement needs a starting point. UT Run - 1, our very first run, was honestly one of the most unforgettable experiences for us. Seeing an idea that started from random conversations actually come to life felt surreal.

The run was held at Bratt, and what made it even more special was the police escort that accompanied us throughout the route. It gave the whole event such an energetic and professional vibe, and seeing everyone run together early in the morning was genuinely exciting.

But it wasn’t just about the run. After crossing the finish line, everyone stayed back for the afterparty, where people got to chill, interact, dance, take pictures, and just enjoy the moment together.

Leveling Up: UT Run - 2

Following an amazing launch, we brought the heat for UT Run - 2. Conducted on the bright morning of June 7th at 6:00 AM, this session turned things up with an energizing 5km route starting and finishing right outside Arise Café.

The turn-out was absolutely electric! The energy kept pounding well past the finish line as the community rallied inside for a high-intensity morning afterparty. It is safe to say the youth culture of Vijayawada has completely embraced early mornings.

Expanding Horizons: UT Run - 3

For UT Run - 3 on the morning of July 19th at 6:00 AM, our community gathered at Crab Café for another massive turnout.

From pre-run stretch sessions under the trees to pounding the pavement together and celebrating back at the café with delicious breakfast refreshments, the energy was through the roof. The community continues to grow stronger with every single step!
